Knife.Picks notes: which pan fits which cook

How we would choose, based on what each brand publishes.

Everyday searing: a stainless clad frying pan lasts for decades.

Eggs and fish: a non-stick pan is easiest; keep the heat medium.

High heat and steak: carbon steel heats fast and gets better as it seasons.

Questions, answered

Short answers based on what each brand lists on its product pages.

Is stainless or non-stick better?

Stainless browns food better and lasts longer; non-stick is easier for eggs and needs replacing every few years.
