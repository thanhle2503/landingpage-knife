Knife.Picks notes: which sharpener fits which cook

How we would choose, based on what each brand publishes.

First proper sharpener: a guided clamp sharpener such as the Precision Adjust holds the angle for you.

Fast and many knives: an electric belt sharpener (Knife & Tool or Ken Onion) does a knife in a minute.

Camping and pocket knives: a field or Pivot sharpener fits in a bag.

In Australia: buy from the Work Sharp Australia store for AUD prices and local warranty; in the US, the Amazon links ship faster.

Questions, answered

Short answers based on what each brand lists on its product pages.

Which Work Sharp is best for kitchen knives?

The Precision Adjust for guided hand sharpening, or the Knife & Tool Sharpener Mk.2 if you want an electric belt.

Why are there two Work Sharp columns?

One is Work Sharp on Amazon US (USD), the other is the official Australian store (AUD). The machines are the same family; pick the one that ships to you.
