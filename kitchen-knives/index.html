Questions, answered

Short answers based on what each brand lists on its product pages.

What size chef's knife should I buy?

An 8-inch (20 cm) chef's knife suits most home cooks. Go shorter if you have small hands or a small board.

German or Japanese style?

German-style blades are thicker and tougher; Japanese-style blades are thinner and sharper but need more care.
